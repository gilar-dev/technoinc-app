## Introduction

Welcome to the official source code of TechnoInc MC Wiki, a self-built encyclopedia for Minecraft survival world for five years!

What you will found on this project:
- A fictional civilizations, organizations, ideologies, figures, literatures, and more.
- An encyclopedia for everything within TechnoInc survival world.
- You can edit or even create your own article based on your own stories or imaginations.
- Non commercial platform and has been indexed by Google

You can check out the site [here](https://technoinc.world) - your feedback and contributions are welcome!

## What is it used for

This is not just a test project, but it's a home for every story, journey, and memory has been there for five years and so on, as the world keeps being played and more lores are getting created everyday. It will grow bigger and bigger!
